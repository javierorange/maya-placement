## What counts as a placement

Keep a role if **all** of these are true:

- Aimed at **current university students** (industrial placement, year in industry, placement year, *stage de césure*, long internship, *Praktikum* 6–12 months).
- Duration is **between 6 months and 12 months** (inclusive). Prefer 9–12 months when Bath International Management expects a year abroad / placement year.
- Start is compatible with a 2027 placement year (typically summer 2027; earlier 2026–27 starts are fine if Maya could actually take them).

Drop:

- Summer internships under ~6 months
- Graduate schemes / new-grad roles
- Part-time campus jobs, volunteering, and unpaid “insight” weeks

Bath BSc International Management usually spends Year 3 **abroad** (work placement, academic exchange, or both). UK roles are still in scope because this search includes the UK.
